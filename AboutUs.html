Who we are

Our story

Kweku Essamuah Acquah Eduful is the founder and Creative Director of Abeiku Edqlics Studio L.L.C.—a premium visual production company specializing in cinematic photography and film across the DMV (Washington, DC, Maryland & Virginia) and Accra, Ghana.

We craft timeless visual stories through event coverage, luxury portraits, corporate branding, promotional films, livestream productions, food cinematography, funerals, and commercial content—delivering every project with precision, creativity, and emotional depth.

From intimate moments to large-scale productions, we transform vision into compelling imagery that leaves a lasting impression.

Available worldwide. Ready to create.
